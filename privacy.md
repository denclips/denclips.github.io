# DenClips — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 21 November 2026**

## 1. About this policy

DenClips respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you authorise DenClips to connect to your TikTok account.

With your permission, DenClips may access basic TikTok profile information and information required to upload video content as drafts.

DenClips only requests information needed to provide its content-management functionality.

## 3. How information is used

Information accessed through TikTok is used to:
- Connect your authorised TikTok account.
- Upload video content as drafts for your review.
- Support the operation and security of the service.

DenClips does not sell personal information.

## 4. Sharing and third-party services

The service uses TikTok's authorised developer integrations. Information may be processed by TikTok in accordance with its own privacy policy and terms.

## 5. Data security and retention

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information. Information should not be retained longer than necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

## 6. Your choices

You can revoke DenClips' access through your TikTok account's authorised app settings. You may also contact DenClips with privacy-related questions.
